title: Porukam
short: Contracts between people and businesses: stages, checklists, payment.
full:
Porukam turns a verbal agreement into a legally meaningful contract and walks the deal step by step, all the way to settlement.

Agreeing in words is easy. Proving later what was agreed, when the work was delivered and what to pay for is hard. Porukam keeps that record for both sides.

A contract in minutes
Pick the deal type: services, sale, rent or a loan. Answer plain-language questions and get a ready document. No legal wording to write.

Stages and checklist acceptance
Split the deal into stages, each with a list of what must be done. The contractor marks items done and attaches photos; the customer accepts or returns the stage with remarks. Both sides always see where the deal stands and whose move is next.

Payment tied to results
Payment follows the stages: accepted means paid. We do not hold your money and charge no fee for signing. Settlements stay between you; the platform records what was accepted and paid, and when.

Evidence that lasts
Photos, files and messages are attached to the stage and sealed with a trusted timestamp. A signed contract never changes except through an amendment both parties see and confirm. If a dispute comes, you have what it takes to close it.

Reputation built on facts
A reputation card is assembled from completed deals, not from reviews anyone can write about anyone.
